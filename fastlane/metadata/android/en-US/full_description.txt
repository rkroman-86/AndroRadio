AndroRadio is a simple and fast internet radio player. Everything fits on one screen, with no menus to dig through.

ONE SCREEN, FIVE BANDS
• Now playing: station, artist, song and album cover
• Recently played: your last 10 stations
• Favorites: 20 slots, reorder them by drag and drop
• Local stations: popular stations of your country, refresh for more
• Controls: one Play/Stop button, Favorites, Search

FIND ANY STATION
Search tens of thousands of stations by name, genre, country, language, region or codec. Duplicates are removed automatically.

LISTEN YOUR WAY
• Background playback with lock-screen and notification controls
• Next / previous buttons (notification, headset, car) switch between your favorites
• Automatic reconnection if a stream drops
• MP3, AAC, OGG and HLS streams
• Phone and tablet layouts

SIMPLE AND PRIVATE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your favorites and history stay on your device.

Available in English, French and Russian.

CREDITS
Station directory: Radio Browser (radio-browser.info), a free, community-run database.
Album art: iTunes Search API (Apple), MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive.
AndroRadio does not host, record or download any content: streams are broadcast by the stations themselves. Station names and logos belong to their owners.
